If you'd like to note the gap, you can add a small editorial note yourself (like "General Conference week — no letter") and we'll typeset it right alongside the others.

Returned Missionary

It's never too late. If you saved the weekly emails — even in an old Gmail folder or exported file — we can work with them. Forward or upload them to your account and we'll sequence everything in order. Families who waited two or three years have still created stunning books. The letters haven't lost any of their meaning.
A partial mission book is still a beautiful, meaningful keepsake. We'll lay out what you have, and you can optionally add a short note for any weeks that are missing. Some families also pull in screenshots from family group chats or notes from their own journals to fill those gaps. We can help you think through what you have — just reach out.
