How It Works

Three steps.
Zero effort from you.

You forward one email address. We handle everything else for the entire mission.

1
Before They Leave
Sign up and forward their mission email address to us.
We give you a forwarding address. Add it to their email list once — that’s the only thing you ever do.
2
Every Week — Whole Mission
Their weekly email arrives. We read it and add it to the book.
Every Monday we receive their email, pull the photos, lay it out, and add it to their growing record. Automatically.
3
When They Come Home
We ship them a hardcover book of their entire mission.
Every week, every area, every companion — printed, bound, and ready the day they walk through the door.
