how it began

How it started

Weekly is a missionary book printing company based out of Salt Lake City, Utah, partnering with a book printer in New York.

It all started with Lucas — when he returned home from two years of weekly mission letters, we wanted to do something meaningful with them — a real book, something worth keeping. But no matter where we looked, we couldn't find a company that produced anything close to the quality we had in mind. Missionary families told us they had the same experience.

So rather than settle, we made it ourselves, and that's when Weekly began.

Lucas, home from his mission. The book that started everything.
