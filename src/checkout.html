A portrait 8x10in hardcover with a matte finish, printed in full color on 80lb gloss paper stock. Every book has unlimited pages and a binding built to last for generations.

Each book is built on a professional design template that opens with a personalized title page and a table of contents. Every weekly entry is automatically labeled with the mission area and week number.

Any photos attached to your missionary’s weekly emails are included automatically. If you’d like us to pull additional photos from a Google Photos link, that’s available as an upgrade add-on at checkout.

Before the weekly emails, you can choose to include:
  • Mission Call
  • Mission Map
  • Farewell Talk
  • Homecoming talk
Once you approve your book layout, printing typically takes 5–7 business days and shipping is an additional 3–5 days depending on your location. Standard shipping is free within the U.S.
