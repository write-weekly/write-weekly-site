Frequently asked questions
Unlike a standard photo book, our Missionary Books preserve the complete mission story, not just the photos. We combine weekly letters, mission maps, talks, milestones, and photos into a professionally designed hardcover keepsake.

We also provide ongoing support throughout the mission, including reminders that encourage missionaries to continue writing and documenting their experiences. The result is a personalized, high-quality record of their mission experience.
Our system automatically includes photos that are attached to your missionary’s weekly emails, so no additional action is required for those images.

For missionaries who store additional mission photos in Google Photos that they want to be included, we also offer a Google Photos upgrade that allows us to import photos directly from your Google Photos library. (Please select this option at checkout.)
Currently serving or departing: Your missionary adds our email address to their weekly email list. Every letter and photo is collected automatically from then on, with photos from the attached email kept alongside the week they were sent and each entry marked with their mission area at the time.

Returned missionary: We send you our email address and you forward the emails you want in your mission book, all at once or a few at a time. Our system will sort them back into order.
Before printing, you’ll receive a digital proof to review. You’ll have the opportunity to verify photos, content, and layout details so you can be confident the finished book reflects your missionary’s experience.
Yes! One of the unique benefits of our service is that we actively follow up with missionaries throughout their mission, encouraging them to keep writing and documenting their experiences. Along with each reminder, we send prompts and ideas to write about, so there’s always a place to start.
Once you approve your book layout, printing typically takes 5–7 business days and shipping is an additional 3–5 days depending on your location. Standard shipping is free within the U.S.
